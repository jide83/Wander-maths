Wander World

Walk the block world. Every few minutes a card appears and asks you to read.

For the parent: put this on the Home Screen
  1. Open this page in Safari on the iPad.
  2. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
  4. Open Wander World from the Home Screen. It runs full screen with no Safari bars.

Hold the gear for three seconds to reach settings. Nothing leaves the iPad: no account, no analytics, no ads, and no network use once the page has loaded.
